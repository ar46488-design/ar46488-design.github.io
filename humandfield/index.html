HumAndField Studio

Complex things, taken apart.

HumAndField Studio is the private, self-hosted publishing tool of the YouTube channel Hum & Field. It runs only on the channel owner's own computer. It uploads the channel's own explainer videos, adds them to the channel's playlists and replies to comments on the channel's own videos, using the YouTube Data API.

It is not offered to the public and has no other users. Nobody else can sign in to it.
